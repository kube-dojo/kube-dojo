# KubeDojo track execution matrix

Status: program planning, based on the 2026-09-04 inventory and the initial [review and expansion plan](../content-review-and-expansion-plan-2026-09-04.md). This covers the whole requested curriculum tree; it is not a claim that every section has been audited.

The source indexes establish the names, routes, and file presence. They do not establish factual correctness, source freshness, accessibility, lab execution, learner performance, translation quality, deployment, or external certification alignment. No external alignment has been verified yet. No absence may be inferred from filenames alone. A section is an **add** candidate only after an evidence-backed audit shows that revision, a bridge, or a fixture cannot close the gap.

The initial audit records these known defects: the structural score returned 5/5 for all 828 English modules; the introductory Zero to Terminal lesson is much longer than its stated quick timing; the Ukrainian introductory AI lesson has audience drift; sampled labs in Kubernetes Basics, Cloud Native 101, Linux troubleshooting, AWS VPC, and Platform Systems Thinking under-test their stated outcomes; and exact-path Ukrainian parity is 0/124 for AI/ML Engineering, 3/242 for Platform, and 0/57 for On-Premises. These are evidence-bounded starting points, not a verdict on every neighboring section. The audit also warns that its heading scans and metadata are triage signals.

## Shared execution contract

For every section, inventory outcomes, prerequisites, route links, factual claims, version/date claims, source links, exercises, accessibility, EN/UK correspondence, and cleanup/reset behavior. Classify each finding as structural, source-verified, editorial, runnable-lab, or learner evidence. Revise when evidence shows an outcome mismatch, unsupported or stale claim, broken route, inaccessible interaction, translation drift, or lab that cannot demonstrate the promised skill. Add only a proven structural or transition gap. Simulated incidents must be labeled; historical incidents and vendor/model capabilities require primary sources. Do not use mystery framing, invented anecdotes, or completion clicks as evidence of mastery.

Source shorthand: **Repo** = the section index, module text/frontmatter, route links, fixtures, and `docs/quality-rubric.md`; **Std** = primary standards, project documentation, man/kernel/IETF/OCI/CNCF material; **Cloud** = current AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure product, pricing, CLI, and managed-Kubernetes documentation; **Tool** = current upstream tool documentation and release notes; **ML** = primary papers and official framework/model documentation; **FinOps** = FinOps Foundation material; **UA** = English source revision plus Ukrainian terminology and translation review; **A11y** = WCAG/WAI-ARIA and keyboard/screen-reader checks; **Learner** = clean-environment execution, observation, transfer, and delayed recall.

## Wave order and completion gates

Advance by acceptance evidence, with no calendar promise. **Wave A** inventories all rows, reconciles old plans, maps claims to source families, and separates known defects from coverage questions. **Wave B** repairs entry routes: Prerequisites, Linux fundamentals, AI Foundations, plus the known introductory and sampled-lab defects. **Wave C** validates operational cores: KCNA/CKA/CKAD/CKS routes, one Cloud provider path, On-Premises Planning/Provisioning, and Platform Foundations/Disciplines. **Wave D** handles breadth and integration: remaining Kubernetes certifications, Cloud enterprise/managed services, Platform Toolkits, On-Premises operations/security/resilience, AI/ML depth, cross-track capstones, and stable Ukrainian routes; translate only after the English route stabilizes. **Wave E** performs independent-family review, focused tests/build and rendered-site checks, route/link checks, and release evidence. The accountable parent owns integration, external issue/PR actions, and final disposition.

Every packet closes with changed paths, evidence reviewed, deterministic commands/results, learner or execution evidence where applicable, remaining risks, and blockers. A green structural heuristic, file count, completion click, or unverified source list cannot close a packet by itself.
