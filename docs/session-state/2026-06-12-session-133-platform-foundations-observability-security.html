Loop refinements & gotchas (new this session)

  • The incident-dedup gate trips on a SOURCE-CITATION TITLE, not just prose. Adding the real AWS-S3-2017 source to 3.1 made the citation title "Summary of the Amazon S3 Service Disruption in US-EAST-1 (February 2017)" match the S3…us-east-1…2017 regex → 3.1 flagged duplicate (canonical owner = reliability-engineering 2.2). Fix per the established convention (cf. distributed-systems 5.1): an <!-- incident-xref: aws-s3-useast1-2017 --> marker in prose within 200 chars + a cross-link to the canonical. Lesson: when you add a real-incident source/citation, re-run the dedup gate — the title can trip it even if the body doesn't.
