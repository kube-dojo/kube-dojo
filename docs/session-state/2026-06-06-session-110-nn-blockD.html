Process notes / gotchas (fold into future briefs)

  • deepseek self-reports are unreliable. Verify every author claim with verify_module.py — do not trust the agent's own "T0 pass" line.
  • Did-You-Know must be bulleted blockquotes (- > **Did You Know?**). The verifier's _count_bullets counts bullet items; bare > **Did You Know?** blockquotes count as 0–1 → structure_did_you_know_4 fails even with four visible callouts.
  • 47 is an anti_leak token. The has_47 check flags a standalone 47 in prose (a known LLM-template artifact). "epoch 47" tripped it; use a different number.
  • The structure_sections_present gate wants 3–5 learning outcomes, not 5–7 (the Block-C/D author shared-rules said "5–7" — a brief bug; D1/D2 used 5 and passed, D3 used 6 and failed). Use exactly 5.
  • Sub-floor → expand-to-T0 → R1 (no wasted review). D3 failed the floor, so it was expanded to T0 first (cursor) and only then got its single cross-family R1 — no R1 burned on an incomplete draft. (Same pattern as 1.4.2 in Block C.)
  • Sibling-grep must be repo-wide, not section-scoped. My first inbound-link grep only covered deep-learning/ + astro.config; check_site_health.py caught two machine-learning/ modules (2.1, 2.2) still pointing at the old slug.
