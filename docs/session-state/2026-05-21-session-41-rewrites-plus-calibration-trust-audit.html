3. Trust audit on v1 calibration data (new this session)

3.1 Single-fixture lanes — the headline trust problem (#1413)

10 of 12 lanes have exactly one fixture; the remaining two have two. A "lane score" is therefore a model's performance on one specific task. The "gpt-5.5 50% code-writing vs gpt-5.4-mini 100%" comparison the user surfaced reduces to "on the parse-dependabot-cooldown Python fixture, gpt-5.5's submission failed pytest while gpt-5-mini's passed" — n=1 anecdote, not a model-capability ranking. v1 calibration is currently a smoke test of the pipeline, not a calibration of the models.

3.2 22.1% of scores are 0.0; ~half from a KNOWN brittle gate class (#1412)

155 of 701 scores are exactly 0.0. 140 come from deterministic scorers (legitimate "model failed the gate" OR known-brittle substring-AND on prose); 15 come from LLM judges returning 0/10. The brittleness was already documented in memory feedback_calibration_gate_brittleness.md ("prose lanes run LLM judge unconditionally; deterministic gates advisory") but the rule was never enforced in code. ~78 of 155 zeros come from gates that fall under that rule:

  • must_mention_recall, ground_truth_findings, routing_accuracy, parameter_grounding, citation_grounding (each ~13–14 zeros)
  • planted_flaw_recall, required_category_ratio

The 15 LLM-judge zeros are ambiguous: scorer code does NOT distinguish "judge graded 0/10" from "judge response unparseable, defaulted to 0".

3.3 Variance report shipped misleading numbers until R1 caught the mixed-scale bug

The stability renderer aggregated 0/1 deterministic gates and 0/10 LLM-judge scores in the same pstdev/min/max. Initial headline shipped: mean stddev 1.797 / p95 4.409 — mathematically impossible on a 0–1 scale. Sonnet R1 caught it in ~3 min. After normalization: mean 0.271 / median 0.366 / p95 0.500. The selector already had _unit_score; renderer just didn't use it. Now extracted to scripts/calibration/v1/__init__.py and shared.

3.4 16 on-disk reports were stale because renderer isn't run after ledger updates (#1416)

The page per-lane/content-review.html showed gemini-3.5-flash-high at 50%; ledger had it at 100% (both gates passing). Investigation: 7 of 12 per-lane reports + 8 of 14 per-model reports + matrix.html were stale. Score timestamps were AFTER the HTML mtime. Cause: the renderer (scripts/calibration/report.py) is not invoked as a post-condition of run_wave.py, run_cell.py, or replicate.py. Fresh render now landed at canonical paths; #1416 has the proposed fix to wire automatic post-condition renders.
