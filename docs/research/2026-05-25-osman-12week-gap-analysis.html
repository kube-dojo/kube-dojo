Sequencing-gap assessment

Kubedojo currently approaches AI engineering heavily from a deployment, infrastructure, and orchestration perspective. A learner progressing through our modules becomes highly proficient at serving (vLLM/SGLang), quantization, vector databases, and MLOps.

However, Osman's 12-week roadmap is fundamentally a "build from scratch" architectural ladder. Our curriculum has a deep-learning/ track that covers backpropagation and neural networks from scratch, but it abruptly stops before building modern Transformer architectures. If a learner follows our natural order, they gain immense practitioner depth in using and serving models, but they will experience a structural gap in understanding the internal architectural wiring (RoPE, MLA, GRPO, SAEs) compared to Osman's curriculum.

We do not have a comparable end-to-end "build the modern LLM stack" track.

Recommended additions

  1. Module on Modern Transformer Architecture: Add src/content/docs/ai-ml-engineering/deep-learning/module-1.10-modern-transformers-rope-and-attention.md. This should close the gap on RoPE, ALiBi, and the evolution of MQA/GQA/MLA.
  2. Module on Reasoning Model RL (GRPO/RLVR): Add src/content/docs/ai-ml-engineering/advanced-genai/module-1.12-reasoning-models-and-grpo.md to follow up on RLHF and specifically cover the RL techniques used by DeepSeek-R1 and similar reasoning models.
  3. Module on Mechanistic Interpretability: Add src/content/docs/ai-ml-engineering/advanced-genai/module-1.13-mechanistic-interpretability-and-saes.md to introduce Sparse Autoencoders and circuit analysis.
  4. Module Extension - Tokenization: Update src/content/docs/ai-ml-engineering/generative-ai/module-1.2-tokenization-text-processing.md to include a "build a BPE tokenizer from scratch" hands-on Python exercise to match the pedagogical depth of the roadmap.

Conclusion: is the 12-week ladder itself a gap?

Yes and no. The topics missing from the 12-week ladder (RoPE, SAEs, GRPO) are genuine content gaps that should be backfilled into our deep-learning/ and advanced-genai/ tracks. However, creating a literal "12-week-llm-stack" sub-track is likely unnecessary and misaligned with kubedojo's pedagogical goals. Kubedojo's current Information Architecture (IA) correctly separates infrastructure (Serving, K8s, Inference) from fundamentals (Deep Learning, Generative AI). Rather than creating a redundant 12-week "build it all" syllabus, we should integrate these missing low-level architectural components as capstone projects within the existing deep-learning track, allowing the synthesis to occur naturally as the learner bridges into advanced-genai.
